01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curiosity,
with direction.

Hi, I’m Kyeong-Rae.
I’m interested in intelligence that makes sense of the world through more than one sense.

I’m an undergraduate in the School of Electrical Engineering at KAIST, expecting to graduate in February 2027. I’m currently a research intern at AMI Lab, advised by Prof. Tae-Hyun Oh.

My research brings together multimodal learning, spatial audio-visual reasoning, and physical consistency. I like asking how what we see and what we hear can help us understand—and trust—what is happening.
