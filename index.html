01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

A few words,
in my own way.

Hi, I’m Kyeong-Rae. I’m curious about how different senses help us understand the same world—and what AI can learn when we bring their signals together.

I’m an undergraduate in the School of Electrical Engineering at KAIST, with a focus on signal processing, expecting to graduate in February 2027. I’m currently a research intern at AMI Lab, advised by Prof. Tae-Hyun Oh.

My starting point is the signal: what it carries, how it changes, and what another sense can add. I’m interested in fusing visual, acoustic, and tactile signals into useful representations. My current work focuses on spatial audio-visual reasoning and physical consistency.
